- (decision) Timed commitments get due_at + late_window_minutes. The word grace is forbidden for this window: Grace Day already means a countable forgiveness token (2/month, grace_days_per_month(), lib/grace.ts, 20260825110000).
- (decision) Monthly repeat is OUT of scope. No month-close layer exists; weekly_quota alone cost 20260820150000_the_week_closes_and_settles.sql. daily and weekly_quota are kept unchanged.
- (decision) Photo proof REPLACES the morning declaration for a timed commitment (hwt75 answer 3b+A-b), it is not an inert backup attached to an appeal.
- (decision) Referee is INVERTED: submitting proof means the day is held by default. The referee never approves; the referee may OBJECT within a window, and only an objection creates a case. Rejected the approval-queue reading because it puts ~90 photos/month on an unpaid friend, which degrades to approve-all by week three.
- (decision) Claim and photo travel separately. The claim keeps the existing offline path (lib/offline-queue.ts, JSON, Storage); the photo does not queue offline. Forced by lib/offline-queue.ts:73 using localStorage + JSON.stringify (~5MB/origin) against a 10MiB image ceiling.
- (decision) hwt75: midnight is death. No photo by end of the local day = Failed Day. Grace Day (2/month) is the only escape. Explicitly rejected extending the photo to the declaration's 3-day window and rejected falling back to the morning question.
- (decision) hwt75: due_at + late_window_minutes may not cross midnight; enforced by a check constraint on commitment. The captured_on = for_day trigger (20260827100000) is left alone because it is correct.
- (decision) hwt75: columns, not a new table. due_at + late_window_minutes go on commitment; declaration gains completion-time and evidence columns. No occurrence table. Rejected occurrence-replaces-declaration (every reader of declaration would need rewriting) and occurrence-alongside-declaration (two tables narrating one event).
- (constraint) declaration_derive_day() hardcodes for_day := (answered_at at HCM)::date - 1, i.e. always yesterday. A same-day claim on a timed commitment would be filed against the wrong day. The trigger must branch on whether the commitment is timed.
- (constraint) settle_day() gates on declaration_deadline(day, morning_hour) = day+3 at morning_hour. A timed commitment's deadline is midnight of its own day, so the deadline becomes per-commitment rather than per-account, or an unanswered timed day stays open for three days.
- (constraint) gate_reminder() must exclude timed commitments the same way it already excludes daily_hours_quota. Their question is not asked the next morning; it died at midnight.
- (constraint) Reminding at a specific minute does not currently work: gate_reminder is enqueued by an hourly cron at :05 (20260819210000). due_at 20:30 would be reminded at 21:05, after the window shut. Needs a per-minute cron or a separate schedule for timed commitments. The outbox worker already runs every minute (20260819183000) and does not change.
- (constraint) Reuse, do not rebuild: bucket appeal-evidence (config.toml:132, private, 10MiB, png/jpeg/heic), table appeal_evidence, its owner-derivation RLS (20260824130000), and the referee's existing viewer (components/referee-appeal-detail.tsx). The work is detaching evidence from appeal_id, not standing up a second store.
- (constraint) captured_on stays derived from file.lastModified, not EXIF. No EXIF dependency exists in this codebase and adding one is out of scope.
- (constraint) A frozen day accepts no new evidence (20260820102000_supersession_freezes_the_day.sql). Evidence arriving after a day is frozen is refused rather than reopening settled money.
- (constraint) AD-1 server is sole judge, AD-4 idempotency key generated at the tap, AD-6 all boundaries Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh and no client-derived storage dates, AD-8 settlement is the single writer of derived state. All four hold unchanged.
- (note) Accepted consequence of midnight-is-death: the offline split protects the claim but not the photo. A late-day commitment done without signal, where signal does not return before midnight, still costs the day. The product must say this at the moment a time is set, not let the user discover it at month end.
- (assumption) One due_at per commitment per day. Multiple scheduled times for a single commitment are not modelled.
- (assumption) Timed applies to cadence daily and weekly_quota only. daily_hours_quota is judged by measured minutes and is already excluded from declarations entirely (20260820140000).
- (assumption) kind = abstain cannot be timed: there is no moment of doing to photograph, and autoChecksPossible() already excludes abstain from every sensor.
- (assumption) Existing commitments stay untimed. due_at defaults to null and null means the current morning-declaration behaviour, unchanged.
- (question) What is the allowed range and default for late_window_minutes? hwt75 was asked and did not name a number.

- (decision) hwt75: the column is due_time, not due_at. Every _at in this schema is a timestamptz (archived_at, answered_at, created_at, auto_check_last_checked_at); this value is a Postgres time - wall clock, no date, no zone. due_at would be the one _at that is not an instant, and now() >= due_at would be a bug that compiles. Supersedes the earlier due_at naming throughout SPEC.md, brownfield.md, lifecycle.md and stories.yaml.
- (decision) hwt75: late_window_minutes ranges 5 to 240 with a default of 30. Below five is unhittable in practice; above four hours a time of day stops meaning one. Zero is refused - a window of zero requires hitting an exact second. This closes the first open question.
- (constraint) The midnight check cannot be written as due_time + interval: Postgres time arithmetic wraps, so 23:30 + 60 minutes yields 00:30 and the constraint would accept exactly the case it exists to refuse. It compares extracted minutes against 1440, half-open so a window ending at exactly 24:00:00 is accepted.
- (constraint) canBeTimed() must be a separate predicate from autoChecksPossible() even though both currently exclude abstain and daily_hours_quota. autoChecksPossible is about sensors; canBeTimed is about whether there is a moment of doing. The match is coincidental and merging them couples two unrelated rules.
