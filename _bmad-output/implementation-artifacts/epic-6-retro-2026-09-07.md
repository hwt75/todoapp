**F1 — CAP-6 is enforced at enqueue and nowhere else, and the gap is real, not theoretical.**
`enqueue_due_time_reminder()` refuses a window that has already opened
(`20260903090000_the_reminder_lands_inside_the_window.sql:322-334`), so nothing is ever *queued*
late. Delivery has no matching bound: `outbox_claim` selects on
`status = 'pending' and not_before <= now()` with no upper limit
(`20260819180000_outbox.sql:176-177`, read at source during this retrospective). A worker that is
down or backed up therefore delivers a due-time reminder whenever it recovers — after the window
has shut, which is the one thing CAP-6's `success:` line says must never happen. Nothing in the
suite or on a device has exercised the late-delivery case in either direction. **Disposition: fix
now** — either an expiry on the row or a delivery-time check, and a regression for it.

**F2 — CAP-6's other half cannot be closed by any evidence this repository can produce.**
The story says so itself (`spec-6-6-the-reminder-lands-inside-the-window.md:574-576`): nothing in
the suite waits for a minute to arrive, wakes the worker, or puts anything on a lock screen. The
five device checks at `:578-587` remain unrun, story `6-6` remains `review`, and
`epic-6-retro-item-41` is the only `open` action item in `sprint-status.yaml`. **Disposition:
carry forward unchanged** — this is the item that keeps the epic unaccepted.

### Divergences from the spec

**D1 — a timed day nobody claimed settles `expired`, and the author is charged without being
told.** `20260829090000_midnight_decides_the_day.sql:479-482` returns `expired` when
`answered < total`; the penalty is minted at `:494-497`; and `:511` (`continue when verdict =
'expired'`) skips the day summary. Verified at source during this retrospective. The behaviour is
deliberate and tested (`tests/6-4-midnight-decides-the-day.sql:348-351`) with a stated reason — a
summary about a day he never answered is the product pretending to know how it went — but the SPEC's
own Success signal says a forgotten evening "fails on its own, **visibly**". The money lands and
that particular message does not. Story 5.2's silence intervention is a separate surface that may
or may not cover it. **Disposition: open question for the maintainer**, not a unilateral fix.

**D2 — a timed commitment may no longer carry an Auto-check.** `20260829090000:1002-1023` plus the
`commitment_time_not_with_auto_check` constraint, mirrored at `lib/commitment.ts:323-330`. No CAP
asks for this. **Disposition: accept and record** — it closes a real ambiguity, and Epic 4's
retrospective item 33 proposed exactly this.

**D3 — evidence retention was built although the change proposal put it out of scope.**
`sprint-change-proposal-2026-09-03.md` R4 marks storage growth "Out of scope for this story"; the
hourly orphan sweeper shipped anyway (`20260907130000`, `supabase/functions/evidence-sweeper/`).
**Disposition: accepted deviation** — the maintainer asked for it directly in the session that built
it, after the same session closed the defect that produced most orphans. The proposal's line is
stale rather than violated; reconcile it.

**D4 — Story 6.9 is scope no approved artifact added.** The 2026-09-03 proposal adds one story,
6.8. `6-9-a-photo-i-can-open-again` is `done` with `components/kept-photos.tsx` and
`lib/evidence.ts:154`, and its own Intent says evidence had been write-only to the author since
Story 6.3. **Disposition: reconcile the proposal to reality** — the work is sound and CAP-3 implies
it, but no artifact records the decision to add it.

**D5 — the SPEC's three Open Questions about objections were answered inside Story 6.7 and never
written back.** `SPEC.md` still carries them open while `20260903140000:150-158,399-440,640-700`
answers all three. **Disposition: reconcile the SPEC.**

**D6 — one column, three readers, three rules — and it cost two follow-up fixes.**
`declaration_derive_day()` read the live `due_time` (`20260828140000:50-53`), settlement read the
historical one (`20260829090000:216`), the morning gate reads live deliberately (`:860-866`), and
the reminder pass mixes both (`20260903090000:333-338` vs `:432-437`). Two of this session's fixes
exist solely to reconcile that split: `20260907100000` (a queued claim judged by what governed its
day) and `20260907120000` (a `coalesce` that could not tell a recorded null from an absent entry).
**Disposition: process lesson** — the "one door" abstraction existed from Story 6.4 onward, and the
divergence was that not every reader was moved through it when it was introduced.
