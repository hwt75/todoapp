RATIONALE FOR WHAT CHANGED
1. White page became a warm cream. The ground is the one thing on every screen; warmth is the cheapest way to make opening it easier.
2. Hairlines went from 0.5px black to 1px warm. Same lightness on the page (1.25:1), but they stop reading as a spreadsheet.
3. Urgent moved from a warm tan to ochre and lost its fill, keeping only an outline and 6px corners. Hue alone was not enough separation from Failed.
4. Two shadows exist: under the tab bar and under the blocking declaration. Both mark a layer above the page, not importance.
5. Caprasimo is used for exactly three things — the wordmark, the debt figure, the timer. It is the display voice; using it for titles would make the app loud.
6. Titles are Figtree 600, not the display face. Sentence case kept.
7. Radius went 8/12 to 16 with a 28px outer frame, per the system. Status labels stay fully round and are now the only fully round thing.
8. Nothing animates. The quota track is a static fill; the timer is a text update.
9. Dark mode is declared, not inverted: a warm near-black at #17140f with its own four state tints, each measured above 7:1.
10. The logo and app icon are the mark you supplied, used as given. It is cooler and glossier than the rest of the system, so it appears only as a mark — never as a fill, a tint or a state colour inside a screen.
